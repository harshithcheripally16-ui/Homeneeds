How this works:
The master icon is drawn once at 512×512.
All other sizes are downscaled copies of the exact same artwork.
After downloading, deploy to Firebase so PWABuilder fetches the same files.

Master Icon Preview (256px)

📋 Exact steps to match PWABuilder

  1. Click "Download All 8 Icons" above
  2. Move all 8 PNG files to HomeNeeds/icons/
  3. Open VS Code terminal and run:
    firebase deploy
  4. Wait for deploy to finish (30–60 seconds)
  5. Open PWABuilder and re-enter your URL
  6. Icons will now match ✅
⚠️ The most common mistake is downloading icons but forgetting to run firebase deploy before checking PWABuilder. PWABuilder reads icons from your live server, not your local folder.
